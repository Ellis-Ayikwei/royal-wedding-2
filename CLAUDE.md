## Writing style

Never use em dashes (—) anywhere: not in code, comments, JSX copy, seed data, commit
messages, docs, or chat replies. Use a colon, a comma, parentheses, or a full stop
instead, and rewrite the sentence if none of those fit. The same goes for en dashes (–)
used as punctuation; an en dash is fine only in a numeric range such as 5:21-31.
